# Human well-being and public trust

[Blue Fission's public mission](https://bluefission.com/about/) calls for technology that supports people, broad access, accountable decisions, and responsible use. Kyber's work should be assessed against those aims using evidence from the affected workflow.

The [Pro-Human AI Declaration](https://humanstatement.org/) provides an external set of questions about human control, agency, accountability, and effects on people. Kyber should use applicable provisions as a review lens for experiments and releases with material effects. This does not replace the destination owner's rules or grant deployment authority. This repository makes no claim of formal signatory status, completed assessment, or general compliance certification.

Before a covered release, the owner should record which provisions apply, what was tested, who reviewed the evidence, and what remains open. A missing review gate holds the covered release or public claim. It is not a judgment about a person's character or the company's overall worth.

Kyber should seek informed human participation, protect privacy and consent, and document how an affected person can correct or withdraw data where the system permits it. Research and failed experiments can contribute to public knowledge after a disclosure review protects participants, clients, security, and private intellectual property. Publication timing needs an accountable owner.
